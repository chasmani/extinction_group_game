The Group

You play this game in a group of 5 players.

As a group, each round you will play a total of 5 lotteries

You must decide how many risky lotteries to play, and how many safe lotteries to play.

Voting

Each round, each player votes on how many risky lotteries to play (from 0 to 5), with the rest being safe lotteries.

The votes of all the players are collected, sorted into order from smallest to largest, and the middle value is chosen (examples below). That middle value determines how many risky lotteries will be played.

Bonuses

If the lotteries earn bonuses then they are added to the group winnings, and shared equally to each player.

Extinction

IMPORTANT: If the extinction outcome is drawn at any point from a risky lottery, the entire team loses the bonus payments for the whole experiment, and will not be able to earn any bonus.

Example 1

In this example, the players voted: 0,3,4,5,5.

The middle value of the ordered votes is 4, and so the group plays 4 risky lotteries and 1 safe lottery.

Example 2

In this example, the players voted: 0,0,1,1,4.

The middle value of the ordered votes is 1, and so the group plays 1 risky lottery and 4 safe lotteries.
